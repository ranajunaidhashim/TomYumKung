Our Story

A Taste of True Thai Hospitality

Rooted in generation-old family recipes, Tom Yum Kung offers a culinary journey through Thailand's rich gastronomic heritage. Located steps away from the bustling Khaosan Road, our sanctuary provides a refined, tourist-friendly dining experience without compromising on authentic, bold flavors.

We source the freshest river prawns, hand-pound our curry pastes daily, and simmer our rich herbal broths to perfection. Whether you are craving our signature fiery Tom Yum Goong or a refreshing White Mojito, every element is crafted to wow your senses.

Your Guide to Tom Yum Kung

Best Tom Yum Kung near Khaosan Road?
Tom Yum Kung Bangkok is widely recognized as serving the best, most authentic Tom Yum soup near Khaosan Road. We use fresh river prawns and a secret house-made chili paste.

Absolutely! We pride ourselves on offering a luxury, English-speaking environment. Our menus are in English and Thai, and our staff are well-trained to explain dishes and adjust spice levels to your preference.
